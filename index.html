Key findings

  • Dinner is the main event. Items ordered climb from 4 PM and hit about 125K at 6 PM, nearly three times the lunch peak of about 45K at noon.
  • Orders run ahead of dashers at peak. At 6 PM there are roughly 1.55 outstanding orders per busy dasher, the widest gap of the day.
  • Supply follows demand closely. Busy dashers and outstanding orders correlate at 0.94, so dasher load reacts to orders almost hour for hour.
  • A few cuisines carry the volume. American, pizza, and Mexican lead, followed by a long tail of niche categories.
  • One order segment signals strain. Cluster 3 holds small orders with unusually high dasher-to-order ratios.
